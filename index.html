Placement
  1. Find the culvert and mark it as 0+28. Mark the deck ends: 0+00 is 28 ft west, 0+52 is 24 ft east. The stakeout panel beside the map has coordinates for all three.
  2. Clear a 16-ft swath on the lane crown from 0+00 to 0+52 if brush is in the way.
  3. Look for overhead wires. Utility poles run along the laneway.
  4. Set B1–B3 first, lengthwise from 0+20 to 0+36, so each end bears on solid lane about 8 ft past the culvert.
  5. Lay the deck crosswise: #1–5 from 0+00 to 0+20, #6–9 on top of the bridge mats, #10–13 from 0+36 to 0+52. Work from the end you come in on and set each mat ahead of the machine.
  6. Ramp the 8-in steps at 0+20 and 0+36 if the machine needs it.
  7. Pull the mats when the job is done, within a year under Massachusetts forestry BMPs, and leave the lane and banks stable.
The ground
  • The lane is a raised, gravel-topped causeway, about 27 ft wide at mid-height with a crown about 12 ft wide. The 16-ft mats sit on the crown and shoulders.
  • The run is close to level, about 4 in of rise and fall over 52 ft.
  • The crown is only 1½ to 2 ft above the brook and the swamp beside it, so expect shallow cover over the culvert.
